PROPOSED CHANGES: LIVE MONITORING AND "AS OF" FOR CASE URGENCY
(Proposal only. DEMO.md and CONTRACT.md have not been edited for this yet.)

------------------------------------------------------------
1. RULE CHANGES
------------------------------------------------------------

No provisional stage.
Urgency is computed once the trace finishes, then kept current by monitoring.

Replace the 24-hour trigger.
With live monitoring we no longer need a timestamp comparison as a stand-in for
"still moving". We observe it. The third act_now trigger becomes: a fresh
outgoing transfer was observed from a case wallet in the last 15 minutes.
Fifteen minutes is a placeholder for investigators to tune.

Urgency levels (first match wins, top to bottom):

  act_now
    - Idle funds at the last check
    - Funds at a VASP deposit address, not yet swept
    - Fresh outgoing transfer in the last 15 minutes

  act_soon
    - VASP identified at confidence 0.70 or higher
    - Amount lost 10,000 or more
    - 2 or more linked complaints

  preserve
    - Everything else

Live monitoring on each case.
Once a case is traced, its leaf and idle wallets go on a watchlist and are
polled for new transfers (placeholder: every 30 seconds). When a new transfer
appears, the system re-runs the pipeline:

  1. Extend the graph from the new transfer.
  2. Re-run attribution and urgency.
  3. Update the "as of" time.
  4. Push an alert.

Urgency can go down as well as up. If idle funds move, act_now may drop to
act_soon or preserve, and the reasons show why.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. CONTRACT CHANGES (target v1.1.0, combined with the pending urgency change)
------------------------------------------------------------

- urgency_as_of (epoch seconds) on TraceResponse and CaseSummary.
  It is the time of the last successful chain check, not the last change, so a
  quiet case still shows a fresh time.

- monitoring on TraceResponse and CaseSummary:
    status             live | stale | off
    last_checked_at    epoch seconds
    poll_interval_sec  number
  "stale" means the last check is older than about twice the poll interval, so
  the badge cannot claim freshness it does not have.

- New alert type URGENCY_CHANGED, added to Alert.type. The message carries the
  old and new level.

- A light seam function next to trace_complaint:
    new_transfers(addresses, since_ts) -> list[Transfer]
  This avoids re-running a full trace on every poll. The alternative is to call
  trace_complaint repeatedly, which is simpler but heavier and hits API rate
  limits sooner. Recommended: add the new function. It must go through the
  contract change process, since the seam signatures are otherwise fixed.

- Fixtures: the hero case gets urgency_as_of, monitoring: live, and a sample
  URGENCY_CHANGED alert.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. DEMO.md CHANGES
------------------------------------------------------------

- Step 7 (case urgency): swap the 24-hour trigger for the 15-minute
  fresh-transfer one, and add the as-of and re-evaluation rules.

- Step 8 (alerts): replace the simulate-only wording with a real watcher. It
  polls the watchlist, calls new_transfers, re-evaluates, and pushes alerts.
  In demo mode, a recorded transaction replay stands in for live data and is
  labeled SIMULATED.

- Step 9 (Case View): the urgency badge shows "as of 14:32:10" and a
  live/stale indicator. A stale case gets a visible warning.

- Section 11 (team split): assign the watcher to B (alerts.py), the
  new_transfers function to A, and update the engine's "stateless" wording to
  allow this one extra function.

- Coverage map, goals, demo script: mention continuous monitoring, and show an
  alert changing the urgency badge live during the demo.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. LIMITS TO STATE
------------------------------------------------------------

- "Live" means polling, so there is always a delay of up to one poll interval
  plus block confirmation time.
- Public APIs have rate limits, so only leaf and idle wallets are watched, not
  the whole graph.
- Live monitoring improves how current the reading is. It cannot make act_now
  mean a freeze will succeed.

------------------------------------------------------------
PLACEHOLDER VALUES (to be tuned by investigators)
------------------------------------------------------------

  Fresh-transfer window ........ 15 minutes
  Poll interval ................ 30 seconds
  Stale threshold .............. about 2x poll interval
  Large amount ................. 10,000
  VASP confidence threshold .... 0.70
  Linked complaints ............ 2 or more
